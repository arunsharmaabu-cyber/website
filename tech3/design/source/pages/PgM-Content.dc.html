05FAQ

Fair questions.

Who writes it?

Our own writers and editors, not a freelancer marketplace. A second editor checks every piece against your real services, prices and service area. You read it before it goes live.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for research and outlines: finding the questions people ask, checking what already ranks, spotting gaps. A person writes every draft, edits it and checks every fact. Nothing publishes until you approve it.

How many pieces a month?

Plans start at [PRICE] a month for [N] pieces, mixed across formats. We would rather publish four pieces that earn rankings than twenty that don't.
