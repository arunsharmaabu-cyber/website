S/05Questions

Quickanswers.

Do I need a storefront?

No. Plumbers, cleaners, movers and other service-area businesses can rank without a public address. You hide your address in Google and list the suburbs you cover. Shops with a storefront show their address on the pin.

How long to reach the top three?

It depends on your category and where you start. Many profiles move within the first 60 to 90 days once the profile and reviews are fixed. Tough markets take longer. We never promise a rank. We show you the map grid every month so you can see the movement.

Do I need to be verified?

Yes. Google verifies by video, phone or postcard. We walk you through it, and we fix suspended profiles and duplicate listings if you have them.
