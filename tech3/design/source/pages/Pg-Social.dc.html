S/05FAQ

Quickanswers.

Do you post for us?

Yes. We plan the month, make the posts, schedule them and answer comments and messages. You approve the plan, and anything sensitive, before it goes live.

Do I need to be on camera?

No. Plenty of our best posts are jobs, tools, before-and-afters and your crew’s hands. If you do want to appear, we keep it to ten minutes and a phone.

Which platforms?

Where your customers already are. For most local businesses that is Facebook and Instagram, with TikTok, LinkedIn or YouTube added when they fit. We add paid social only when it makes sense.
