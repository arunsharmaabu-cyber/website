05FAQ

Straight answers.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

No. Nobody controls what an AI says. We improve the sources and facts these assistants rely on, then check 100 prompts every month so you can see what moved.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO ranks your pages in search results. GEO gets your business named inside an AI’s written answer. Both start with accurate, trusted information, but the work is different.

How long until we see mentions?

Often a few months. Some assistants read the live web and change fast. Others update slowly. Your first report is a baseline, so every change after it is visible.
