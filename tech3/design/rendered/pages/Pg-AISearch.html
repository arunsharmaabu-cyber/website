S/05FAQ

Quickquestions.

Is this just schema?

No. Schema is one part. We also rewrite pages to open with a direct answer, match your name, address and phone everywhere, open the right doors in robots.txt and speed up mobile. Markup on a messy page does very little.

Will it hurt my current rankings?

It should not. We add markup and structure, and we do not remove what ranks today. We test changes on a few pages first and watch Search Console before rolling them out.

How fast do AI Overviews pick it up?

Google re-reads changed pages in days to a few weeks. Whether an AI Overview then quotes you depends on the question, your competitors and how clear your page is. We can not promise a spot, and nobody honestly can.
